About me

Hi there! I'm Alberto, a composer based in Valencia, Spain, and a lifelong gamer.

What I do

  • Fantasy music for videogames: RPGs, dark fantasy and Souls-like games, medieval worlds, orchestral folk and ethnic-inspired settings
  • A deep command of the subgenres within them, to match the exact tone your game needs
  • The games I’ve worked on follow this same line: fantasy, dark fantasy and ethnic-inspired

Influences

Skyrim, Elden Ring, Golden Sun, Ghost of Tsushima, The Witcher, Game of Thrones, The Lord of the Rings

My approach

The music serves the developer's vision. My job isn't to write memorable music for its own sake: it's to bring to life what the developer hears in their head when they imagine the game.
