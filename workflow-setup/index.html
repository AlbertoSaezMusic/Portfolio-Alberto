Workflow & setup

This is how a project runs when you work with me, whether we do it in person or remotely.

Before we start

Creative Questionnaire

Before working on any track, we start with a creative questionnaire, a kind of assessment of the project's sonic palette. It helps me understand the developer's references and the overall tone the game should have.

Track by track

For each track, the developer gives me specific instructions, and every track then follows the same four steps.

  1. 1. Idea and sketch

    I begin with a very basic MIDI version in which the instrumental lines and the main idea can be heard, even if it sounds rough compared to the final result. The purpose is to validate the sketch before committing to the final version. I send it to the developer as a WAV, and once it is approved, the real work begins.

  2. 2. Orchestration and arrangement

    Here the sketch becomes the actual piece. I work on dynamics, layers, expression, realism and depth, building the full orchestration on top of the approved idea.

  3. 3. Mixing

    Once the arrangement is finished, I export the stems and move on to the mix: level normalization, EQ, reverb and whatever else the track needs.

  4. 4. Delivery

    I deliver the music in an implementation-ready format, together with implementation instructions whenever they are needed.

Day to day

Communication and files

Day-to-day communication usually happens over Discord, and we share all files through a shared Google Drive folder.
